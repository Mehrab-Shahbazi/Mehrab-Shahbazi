## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🔐 Enigma Machine Simulator

A simple Enigma machine simulator with rotor, reflector, and stepping-mechanism support.

#### Features

* Rotor and reflector simulation
* Double-stepping behavior
* Case-insensitive message encoding
* Support for encoding and decoding messages

#### Usage

Initialize the `EnigmaMachine` with rotor and reflector configurations, then use `encode_message()` to process a message.

## 📚 Research & Academic Work

My experience lies at the intersection of:

**Artificial Intelligence × Computer Vision × Deep Learning × Medical Imaging**

I am particularly interested in developing reliable and automated AI methods for medical imaging, including **image quality assessment, quality control, anomaly detection, and robust analysis of MRI data**. The article “[Automated quality control of small-animal MR](https://direct.mit.edu/imag/article/doi/10.1162/imag_a_00317/124612/Automated-quality-control-of-small-animal-MR)” highlights the importance of using machine learning to identify image-quality issues in small-animal magnetic resonance imaging and improve the reliability and efficiency of imaging workflows. This work is associated with the [Aswendt Lab](https://github.com/Aswendt-Lab), whose research further reflects the application of computational methods to advanced biomedical imaging.

This direction aligns with my interest in applying computer vision and deep learning to real-world scientific and biomedical problems, particularly where automated quality control can support reproducible research, reduce manual inspection, and improve downstream image analysis.
